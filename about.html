About Giveaway Pulse

Giveaway Pulse is a free tracker for live YouTube giveaways from creators in India and around the world – phones, consoles, laptops, gadgets and more. Instead of searching through dozens of videos, you can see the prize, the last date, how to enter and, later, the announced winners in one place.

How listings are collected

Giveaways are discovered from public YouTube videos and refreshed every hour. Every listing links back to the creator’s official video, which is always the final source of rules and results. If a creator changes the rules, the video wins over our summary.

We are not the organiser

Giveaway Pulse does not run, sponsor or judge any giveaway listed here, and we never collect entries. We do not charge anything to browse or enter.

Stay safe from fake giveaways

  • A real giveaway never asks you to pay a fee, “delivery charge” or tax to receive a prize.
  • Never share an OTP, password or bank details with anyone claiming you won.
  • Winners are announced by the creator on their official channel – be careful with messages from look-alike accounts on Telegram, WhatsApp or Instagram.
