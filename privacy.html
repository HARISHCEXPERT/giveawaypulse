Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information Giveaway Pulse (giveawaypulse.com) collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account to browse giveaways, and we do not collect your name, email or phone number on the public site. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) for security and performance.

Giveaway Studio

Giveaway Studio runs entirely in your browser. Comments, names and entries you paste into it are processed on your device and are not uploaded to or stored on our servers. The OBS overlay link contains only the giveaway title, the winner name(s) and the entry count that you choose to share.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors’ cookies at aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses data from partner sites.

Third-party links

Listings link to YouTube and to creators’ own pages. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices.

Children

Giveaway Pulse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
